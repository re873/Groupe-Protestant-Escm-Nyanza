Welcome to Our Fellowship

We are a Christ‑centered student community in Rwanda (Southern Province, Nyanza District, Busasamana Sector). Where we act in ECOLE DES SCIENCES SAINT LOUIS DE MONTFORT NYANZA Together we worship, grow through Scripture, and serve our schools and neighborhoods. Explore our story, choirs, leaders, and events — and join us as we follow Jesus with joy and excellence.

Our Mission

To nurture students into mature disciples of Christ through prayer, worship, Bible study, mentoring, and community service — raising servant leaders who influence society with love and truth.

Our Vision

A generation of students rooted in Scripture and excellence, shining the light of Christ in Rwanda and beyond through character, compassion, and bold witness.
