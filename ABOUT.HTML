Who We Are

We are a student‑led Protestant fellowship committed to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and service. We meet weekly for prayer and Bible study, organize outreach, and help students grow as faithful leaders in school and society. Our values are unity, humility, integrity, accountability, and excellence.

History & Focus

Rooted in Scripture and guided by the Holy Spirit, the group has supported students for years through mentorship, music ministry, and community impact. We collaborate with local churches and campus ministries to serve Nyanza and beyond. Our focus includes spiritual growth, leadership development, worship arts, and compassionate service to those in need.
